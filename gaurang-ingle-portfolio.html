AI Tutor — Student Attention, Emotion & Learning Intelligence System
Built a full-stack AI tutor platform that monitors student engagement during recorded lectures via real-time behaviour detection (attentiveness, eye contact, smile, neutral, drowsy). If drowsiness detected, the video auto-pauses and prompts a break. After each lecture, the system generates spoken Q&A — the student answers verbally, and AI evaluates confidence, accuracy, grammar, and areas to improve.
